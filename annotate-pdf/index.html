Mark Up a PDF Like on Paper

Pick a tool, choose a colour and draw straight on the page: highlight a sentence, circle a figure, point an arrow at a detail, scribble a quick note in the margin or pin a sticky note with a comment. Undo the last mark or clear the page whenever you like.

Highlights, pen strokes and shapes are drawn into the page so they look the same in every PDF viewer. Sticky notes are saved as real PDF comments, so Acrobat, Preview and the browser viewers show them as note icons you can open.

Good to Know

Your original page content stays untouched and selectable; the marks sit on top. To remove marks later, mark up from the original file again. Combine annotating with signing and stamping in the PDF Workspace.

Annotate PDF — Frequently Asked Questions

Are sticky notes real PDF comments?

Yes. They are written as standard PDF text annotations with their own icon, so other PDF programs can open and read them.

Will highlights hide the text under them?

No. Highlights are semi-transparent so the words stay readable.

Can I annotate every page?

Yes. Use the page box to move through the document; marks are kept per page until you download.

Does the file leave my device?

No. Everything runs in your browser. Nothing is uploaded.
