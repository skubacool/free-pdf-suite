A pocket scanner that never uploads

Scanner apps want you to install something, create an account, and quietly upload your documents to their servers - then charge you to remove a watermark. This does the same job in a browser tab: point your phone at a page, capture it, and get a clean PDF back. Nothing is uploaded, because there is no server to upload to.

Each page is auto-cropped and perspective-corrected, so a photo taken at an angle comes out square, then cleaned with an adaptive threshold that removes shadows and uneven lighting and leaves crisp black text on white. Capture as many pages as you need and export them as one multi-page PDF.

How to use it

  1. On your phone, tap Use camera and allow camera access - or drop in photos you already took.
  2. Capture each page. They appear in a list you can reorder, rotate or remove.
  3. Pick the clean-up style and page size, then tap Create PDF and download.
🔒 100% private. Camera frames and photos are processed entirely in your browser - they are never uploaded to any server.

Already have a scan that needs shrinking? Use Compress PDF. Need the text searchable afterwards? Try OCR PDF.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to install an app?

No. It runs in your mobile browser. Tap Use camera, capture your pages and download the PDF - nothing to install and no account.

Are my documents uploaded anywhere?

No. Camera frames and photos are processed entirely in your browser, so contracts, IDs and bank statements never leave your phone. Most scanner apps upload to their servers; this one cannot, because there is no server.

What does the clean-up actually do?

Auto-crop finds the page and corrects the perspective from holding the phone at an angle, then Black and white applies an adaptive threshold so uneven lighting and shadows disappear and text goes crisp - like a flatbed scan.

Can I scan several pages into one PDF?

Yes. Capture or add as many pages as you like, reorder or rotate them in the list, then export them all as a single multi-page PDF.

The auto-crop cut the wrong area - what now?

Untick Auto-crop to keep the full photo, or retake the shot with the whole page visible against a contrasting surface. Auto-crop deliberately keeps the whole frame whenever it is not confident.
